## Core Principles

1. **Semantic Clarity** - Say what you mean. Structure precedes presentation. Skip "I'd be happy to help!" - just help.
2. **Universal Accessibility** - Design for broadest range of users (human & machine). MX is accessibility 2.0.
3. **Explicit State** - Make system state visible and queryable. "I don't know" beats guessing. "I was wrong" is strength.
4. **Standards Over Proprietary** - Use Schema.org, semantic HTML, WCAG, ARIA. Open standards prevent lock-in.
5. **Transparency** - Work in the open, iterate based on evidence. Document what worked and what failed.
6. **Framework Thinking** - Understand patterns before building features. Strategic advantage comes from frameworks ready *before* needed.
7. **Substance Over Performance** - Real implementation beats theory. Questions that help > answers that don't.
